How it works

You plan the route. The guardian watches it.

  1. Mark where you are going. Tap your start and destination on the map. SafeRoute draws the walking route.
  2. Leave the route and the bot calls. More than 150 m off course, and the guardian bot rings and asks: are you safe?
  3. Tell it why. Say why you chose this place. It is marked on your map, so you are not asked again for the same stop.
  4. Show your face. The camera checks it is really you before the trip continues. Changing your destination works the same way.

Emergency

Three missed calls, and help is on the way.

If you do not pick up three calls, the nearest police station and your family get a message with your live location on a map.

If you answer and say “I am not safe”, or press SOS, they are alerted at once. Your camera opens and stays open. It closes only when it recognises your face and hears you say “I am safe now”. Your family then gets a message that you are safe.

Privacy

Your face never leaves your phone.

Face recognition runs on the phone itself. SafeRoute keeps a list of 128 numbers that describe your face, not a photo. Your routes and marked places stay on your phone too. Your location is sent only in an alert.

Setup

Ready in two minutes.

Open the app, go to Setup, and add your name, your phone number and your family contacts. Enroll your face with the camera or a selfie. Then plan a route and start your trip.

This is a working prototype. Out of the box it runs in demo mode and sends nothing. Real calls and SMS need a Twilio account and the SafeRoute server. Alerts to police need an agreement with local police or the 112 emergency system.
